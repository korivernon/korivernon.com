So you have found the secret sauce…

My habanero hot sauce (which isn't such a huge secret).

The idea behind the hot sauce is that your food is already seasoned — you don't need to add any extra seasoning if you want to add a little kick. The result is my habanero hot sauce.

Use

  • Be very cautious when opening!!!! Use a paper towel when opening for the first time, and open over the sink!
  • Refrigerate
  • Shake well before use
  • (Please) Use with caution!
