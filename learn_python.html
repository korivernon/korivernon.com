Learn Python

Let's learn Python! An introduction to computer science adapted from NYU's CS-1114.

Computer Science is not the easiest thing to get the hang of. At times it can be difficult and you may feel like you hit a dead end; but see it through! The curriculum has been adapted from CS-1114 Introduction to Python: Programming and Problem Solving at New York University. It is designed to give students an introduction to Computer Science.

As a Teaching Assistant for CS-1114, it was my goal to make sure students were adequately challenged, prepared, and felt comfortable with the course material.

If you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to me through email!
